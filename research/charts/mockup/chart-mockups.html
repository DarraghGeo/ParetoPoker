These are mock-ups on real solutions, not finished designs. The strips are cut into 40 equal slices of the range; the map uses fixed cuts (70% and 40% equity; more or less movement than the middle hand). In the experiments the line carried 69 to 88% of what hands do facing a bet, three bands carried 74% of river betting, and a six-region map carried 66% of flop betting when its cuts were fitted to each spot; with the fixed cuts drawn here this flop reaches 46%. No picture tested implied bet size.
