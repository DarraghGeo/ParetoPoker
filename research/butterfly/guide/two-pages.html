Bet, check, call or fold: twelve spots on one page

After the flop, with two players. Find the spot, find your hand, do what the cell says. Made from 6731 solver decisions and tested on boards it had not seen.

1. The price of a call

Call ÷ (pot with his bet in it + your call).

He bets a third of the pot: 20%. Half: 25%. Three quarters: 30%. The pot: 33%. Twice the pot: 40%.

2. Your equity

How often you win against all the hands he can hold if the rest of the cards were dealt with no more betting. River: the share of his hands you beat. Earlier: that share, plus about 2 points per card that makes you the best hand, per street to come.

Equity vs price = equity against the hands he bets with, minus the price.

3. Four things to notice

Stack: the smaller stack left, in pots. Draw: flush draw; open-ended (8 cards make a straight); gutshot (4 cards). A pair can also be a draw. The flop: who bet it. His weak hands: how much of what he can hold has under 30% equity against what you can hold.

Flop, first to act, out of position: check every hand (loses 0.11% of the pot per decision). Either = both choices are worth the same. Hand names: top pair pairs the highest board card; middle pair pairs the second, or is a pocket pair between the top two; overpair is a pocket pair above the board; trips or better includes straights and flushes. Limits: losses are measured against solver play with equity known exactly. Six-handed cash, 100 big blinds. No bet sizes or raises: as a default bet a third of the pot on the flop and three quarters later. Flop tables rest on few decisions.

Specification for an engine

Reproduces page one exactly. Output BET or CHECK when no bet is faced, CALL or FOLD when one is. No sizing, no raises.

Scope. No-limit hold'em, two players after the flop, six-handed cash ranges, 100 big blinds. Decision nodes: each player's first decision on a street, and facing a bet or raise.

Inputs. Board; hero's two cards; hero's and villain's ranges at this node as a weight per two-card combination, after all actions so far including the bet faced; pot including all bets; amount to call; effective stack behind; betting history.

equity(hand) = expected share of the pot against villain's range at this node with remaining board cards dealt and no further betting; weight villain's combinations by range weight; drop combinations sharing a card with hero's hand or the board; a tie is half. Bands: <0.20; [0.20,0.35); [0.35,0.50); [0.50,0.65); [0.65,0.80); ≥0.80.

price = toCall ÷ (pot + toCall). margin = equity − price. Bands: <−0.05 "5+ under the price"; [−0.05,0) "0-5 under"; [0,0.05) "0-5 over"; [0.05,0.15) "5-15 over"; ≥0.15 "15+ over".

row, first that applies (board ranks = distinct ranks on board, highest first):

  1. trips or better: five of a suit among hole+board with ≥1 hole card of that suit and <5 of it on board; or best straight from hole+board is higher than best straight on board alone; or a hole card's rank occurs ≥3 times among hole+board.
  2. two pair: hole cards of different ranks, each matching a board rank.
  3. pocket pair: above top board rank → overpair; else ≥ second board rank → middle pair; else low pair.
  4. one hole card matches a board rank: top → top pair; second → middle pair; else low pair.
  5. draw (flop, turn): exactly four of a suit among hole+board including a hole card; or ≥2 distinct ranks would each give a higher straight than now.
  6. air.

draw class (any hand, flop and turn; river = no draw): flush draw if 9 cards complete a flush using a hole card; else open-ended if ≥8 cards complete a straight; else gutshot if 4–7 do; else no draw.

stack to pot = stack behind ÷ pot. Bands: <2 "under 2"; [2,6) "2 to 6"; ≥6 "over 6".

his weak hands = share by weight of villain's range with equity against hero's range <0.30. Bands: <0.20; [0.20,0.45); ≥0.45.

who bet last street = last player to bet or raise on the previous street: "I did", "he did", or "nobody" (checked through).

Procedure. Spot = street × seat × (bet faced?). Compute the spot's inputs, look up the cell. Letters: B bet, x check, C call, f fold. A cell shown as Either on page one takes the action listed here. Rows below run weakest to strongest; columns in the order listed.

Check. On solved spots, per decision: Σ over hero's range of weight × (solver value of best action − value of the engine's action) ÷ pot. Value a bet as the solver's own mix of sizes for that hand (best size if it never bets); same for call against raise. Average over decisions. Expected loss, % of pot, is given per table (five folds by spot, 41 spots; trees: bets 33/75/125% flop, 75% turn and river, raises 3×).
