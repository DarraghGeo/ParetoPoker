Bet, check, call, raise or fold: twelve spots on one page

After the flop, with two players. Find the spot, find your hand, do what the cell says. Two answers in a cell: how often to take each, out of 100.

1. The price of a call

Call ÷ (pot with his bet in it + your call).

He bets a third of the pot: 20%. Half: 25%. Three quarters: 30%. The pot: 33%. Twice the pot: 40%.

2. Your equity

How often you win against all the hands he can hold if the rest of the cards were dealt with no more betting. River: the share of his hands you beat. Earlier: that share, plus about 2 points per card that makes you the best hand, per street to come.

Equity vs price = equity against the hands he bets with, minus the price.

3. Four things to notice

Stack: the smaller stack left, in pots. Draw: flush draw; open-ended (8 cards make a straight); gutshot (4 cards). A pair can also be a draw. The flop: who bet it. His weak hands: how much of what he can hold has under 30% equity against what you can hold.

Raise = three times his bet. Two answers in a cell are worth the same there; the split is the solver's. Range equity: the average equity of all the hands you can hold. Hand names: top pair pairs the highest board card; middle pair pairs the second, or is a pocket pair between the top two; overpair is a pocket pair above the board; trips or better includes straights and flushes. Limits: made from 13,146 solver decisions with raises on every street, tested on flops not used to make it; losses are against solver play with equity known exactly. Single-raised and 3-bet pots, 100 big blinds. About a quarter of this play still differs from the solver's, hand by hand. Bet sizes are not chosen: bet a third of the pot on the flop, three quarters later.

Specification for an engine

Reproduces page one exactly. Output CHECK or BET when no bet is faced; FOLD, CALL or RAISE (to 3× the bet faced, or all-in if that is all the tree offers) when one is; where a cell mixes, draw the answer at random with its weights. No bet sizing.

Scope. No-limit hold'em, two players after the flop, 100 big blinds; single-raised pots (BU, CO or UTG against BB) and 3-bet pots (SB against BU). Every decision reached, within its street, 3% of the time or more: first to act, checked to, facing a bet, facing a raise.

Inputs. Board; hero's two cards; hero's and villain's ranges at this node as a weight per two-card combination, after all actions so far including the bet faced; pot including all bets; amount to call; effective stack behind; betting history.

equity(hand) = expected share of the pot against villain's range at this node with remaining board cards dealt and no further betting; weight villain's combinations by range weight; drop combinations sharing a card with hero's hand or the board; a tie is half. Bands: <0.20; [0.20,0.35); [0.35,0.50); [0.50,0.65); [0.65,0.80); ≥0.80.

price = toCall ÷ (pot + toCall). margin = equity − price. Bands: <−0.05 "5+ under the price"; [−0.05,0) "0-5 under"; [0,0.05) "0-5 over"; [0.05,0.15) "5-15 over"; [0.15,0.30) "15-30 over"; ≥0.30 "30+ over".

row, first that applies (board ranks = distinct ranks on board, highest first):

  1. trips or better: five of a suit among hole+board with ≥1 hole card of that suit and <5 of it on board; or best straight from hole+board is higher than best straight on board alone; or a hole card's rank occurs ≥3 times among hole+board.
  2. two pair: hole cards of different ranks, each matching a board rank.
  3. pocket pair: above top board rank → overpair; else ≥ second board rank → middle pair; else low pair.
  4. one hole card matches a board rank: top → top pair; second → middle pair; else low pair.
  5. draw (flop, turn): exactly four of a suit among hole+board including a hole card; or ≥2 distinct ranks would each give a higher straight than now.
  6. air.

draw class (any hand, flop and turn; river = no draw): flush draw if 9 cards complete a flush using a hole card; else open-ended if ≥8 cards complete a straight; else gutshot if 4–7 do; else no draw.

stack to pot = stack behind ÷ pot. Bands: <2 "under 2"; [2,6) "2 to 6"; ≥6 "over 6".

his weak hands = share by weight of villain's range with equity against hero's range <0.30. Bands: <0.20; [0.20,0.45); ≥0.45. range equity = hero's range-weighted mean equity. Bands: <0.45; [0.45,0.55); ≥0.55.

who bet last street = last player to bet or raise on the previous street: "I did", "he did", or "nobody" (checked through).

Procedure. Spot = street × seat × (bet or raise faced?). Compute the spot's inputs, look up the cell. A cell holding under 0.3% of the range in the data takes the answer of the next band up in its column. Each cell is three digits: quarters of the time to give the first, second and third answer (first to act: check, bet, bet big over the pot; facing: fold, call, raise). 031 = call three times in four, raise one in four. A cell plays its least-losing answer, and any answer within 0.5% of the pot per hand of it, in the solver's proportions. Rows below run weakest to strongest; columns in the order listed. Where the tree offers no raise, R is played as C.

Check. On solved spots, per decision: Σ over hero's range of weight × (solver value of best action − value of the engine's answer) ÷ pot. An answer covering several actions (bet sizes up to the pot; raise or all-in) is valued as the solver's own mix of them for that hand, or the best of them if the solver never takes it. Average over decisions. Expected loss, % of pot, is given per table (five folds by flop). Solves: poker-huerosolver fullhand for the flop and turn (226 flops; bets 33/75% flop, 75% turn, all-in, one 3× raise a street) and handselector-river-real for the river (300 rivers on 72 flops; bets 33/75/150%, all-in, two 3× raises), solved to 0.3% of the pot.
