# General

Bluffs:

| Flop | Turn | River |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Any hand, when you bet B33 | Draws | s ÷ (1 + s) per value bet, from hands that can't win a showdown |

| **Value threshold by bet size** | B33: flop 70%, turn 50%, river 75%. B75: 80%. B150: 85%. |
| --- | --- |

The price of a call is bet ÷ (pot + 2 × bet).

## First to Act

### Out of Position

Your default here is to check. Given the disadvantage that OOP has we always play a little bit more cautious from this position.

The exception is when we have range advantage (any more top pair or better than villain). Then on the flop bet value and draws, and on the turn bet anything with 50% equity or a draw. They can mix. The hands that certainly can't bet are the middling ones with no draw.

On the river, check everything if villain's range is strong (a fifth or more of it beats most of yours). Otherwise bet value and bluff your weakest hands.

Out of position, simplify to one bet size: B33 on flop and turn, B75 on the river.

### In Position

Your default here is to bet B33.

On the flop bet your whole range. On the turn bet anything with 50% equity or a draw, and check the rest. On the river, bet value and add bluffs by the ratio, with no bluffs if villain has few weak hands to fold.

If you have nut advantage (top 2 rows) you can add a B75 size, if you've a strength advantage (top 3 rows) you can add B150.

Top row bets up to the B150 if available, second row bets up to the B75 if available, and other value rows bet B33. They only bet pure their biggest size if they've strong blockers to hands that beat them and unblock villain's strong hands that they beat.

All bluffs mix, with the best bluffs (by draw equity and blockers) including the larger bets.

## Facing Aggression

Raise with your absolute strongest (top bar) and pick bluffs based on blockers and draw equity. If a bluff has both great draw equity and great blockers, raise it pure. Otherwise mix. On the river, widen the value raises to any hand that beats three quarters of his betting range.

Continue with hands that have enough equity: in position, the price on the turn and river and 5% more on the flop; out of position, 10–15% more than the price before the river. A draw counts for about 10% extra equity on the turn and 17% on the flop. Nut draws continue pure.

On the river, and in position on any street, if that leaves you short of MDF, fill up to it with your next-best hands. Out of position before the river, don't fill up to MDF with weak draws; folding more than MDF is correct there.
